# Story calendar: the 2026–27 season

This is the release calendar for the story series (design in `STORIES_DESIGN.md`; index in `README.md`). It also lists the real-world dates the stories can reflect. Each level's weekly arcs and briefs live in `STORIES_<LEVEL>.md` (`STORIES_A1.md`) and take their dates from here.


## Principles

- **One global calendar, one track.** Every learner gets the same story on the same date. There is no sequential lock.
- **Weekly rhythm.** Episodes Monday to Friday, a special at the weekend: about 6 a week, about 50 per level.
- **Synced to a TÖMER year.** A1 runs Sept–Oct, A2 Nov–Dec and B1 Jan–early Mar, with a week off between levels and no stories in those weeks. The series may reach 200+ stories in total. Dates and real-world events are below.
- **Real calendar inside the stories.** Seasons, rain and first snow, 29 Ekim Cumhuriyet Bayramı, 10 Kasım, New Year, exam weeks and Ramazan and more happen in the world when they happen outside it.
- **Late joiners.** They get the full archive of everything already released, in any order. Today's story is highlighted, and *My level* filters suggestions. A learner above the current season reads the archive.
- **One launch** (decided 2026-10-08), *proposed, not committed,* for Mon 9 Nov 2026: the launch date is an open decision (`ROADMAP.md` M9). This term's cohort started A1 on 7 Sep, and A2 starts on 9 Nov. On launch day all 48 A1 stories are released together as the archive, and daily release starts with A2's first episode. From then on, one story is released per day, in step with the course.

## Decided (2026-10-08)

- **The cohort.** A1 runs Mon 7 Sep to Sat 31 Oct 2026, 8 weeks, with the A1 exam in week 8. A break week follows (2–7 Nov), and A2 starts **Mon 9 Nov**.
- **One launch.** All 48 A1 stories (`s1e01`–`s1e48`) are released together as the archive, and daily release starts the same day with A2 (`s2e01`). Nothing is published before then. The date, Mon 9 Nov 2026, is *proposed, not committed*: on 2026-10-10 the owner decided that the roadmap has no dates, and the launch date is an open decision (`ROADMAP.md` M9).
- **Weekly rhythm.** Episodes come out Monday to Friday and the special on **Saturday**. Sunday has no release.
- **Break weeks have no stories.** The calendar skips them, and the archive does not notice the gap.
- **Buffer.** From launch on, at least 2 weeks (12 stories) are written, checked and loaded ahead of their release dates.
- **Re-dating.** If the school's dates differ, only `releaseDate` changes. Ids never do.

*Proposed:* a story is released at 00:00 Istanbul time (UTC+3 all year; Turkey has no daylight saving), so every learner gets the same story on the same day wherever they are.

*Working assumption, to re-check when the school announces it:* A2 ends with the week of 28 Dec–2 Jan, followed by a break from 4 to 9 Jan. B1 runs Mon 11 Jan to Sat 6 Mar 2027, 8 weeks. This follows A1's pattern of a level plus a week off. Beykent's 2025–26 calendar had 8-week modules (1 Sep–23 Oct, 3 Nov–25 Dec, 5 Jan–4 Mar).
