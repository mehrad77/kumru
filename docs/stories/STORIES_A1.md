# Season 1 (A1): days and story briefs

Index: `README.md`. Design rules: `STORIES_DESIGN.md`. World: `STORIES_WORLD.md`. Cast: `STORIES_CAST.md`. Calendar: `STORIES_CALENDAR.md`. Data: `STORIES_SCHEMA.md`.

**Status (2026-10-09): all 48 briefs decided.** The first 47 were reviewed as a season and revised the same day (*Season threads* below). A second review, against Truby's *Anatomy of Story*, gave Nima an arc (*Season arc* below): s1e40 was replaced, s1e48 was written, and a dozen briefs gained a beat. s1e01 is written, as a draft in `sahne/packages/sahne-story/drafts/s1e01/`; the other 47 are briefs only. Next: the owner's review of the briefs, the open characters' details (`STORIES_CAST.md` §3), then writing the stories from the briefs.

**How A1 is planned (decided 2026-10-09).** The earlier weekly arcs and premises are dropped. Each day's story is decided in turn, from four questions to the owner, and its brief is written below.

**Follow the class.** The owner's TÖMER class meets Monday to Thursday and does one unit of its A1 book a week. It starts straight with Ünite 1, so Başlangıç is not taught.
- **Week N is Ünite N** for weeks 1–6. Weeks 7 and 8 review all of A1, with the A1 exam on Fri 30 Oct. What the class does in those weeks is still to be confirmed.
- **What a story may use:** any grammar from its week's unit and earlier. For words, a word is allowed if its word list (books KELİME LİSTESİ or workbook list) is from that unit or earlier, **or** if the books or workbook pages of Ünite 1–N use it. Başlangıç doesn't count, because the class skipped it. For example, numbers 1–20 are on Unit 2's list but practised in Unit 1 B (p. 18), so they are fine in week 1; *lira*, *kaç* and *ne kadar* are not. This is the known-word source for the 95% rule. Anything else is a glossed chunk or a story-only word.
- **Target words** come from the week's own unit, so each story teaches what the class is learning that week.
- Where each word and lesson first appears in the books is mapped in `STORIES_SYLLABUS_A1.md`.

**Calendar:** week 1 starts Mon 7 Sep 2026 and A1 ends Sat 31 Oct. The whole season is released together as the archive at launch, proposed for Mon 9 Nov (`STORIES_CALENDAR.md`). Episodes run Monday to Friday and the special on Saturday; Sunday has no release.

**Cast in a story:** at most 4 speakers in an episode and 6 in a special. Silent classmates in the background are `extras`.

**Week threads** (decided as each week starts):
- Week 1: Nima arrives, registers, has a first lesson and a first free day; welcome dinner.
- Week 2: Nima looks for a flat, which will be in **Feriköy** (decided in s1e08). New voices: Yusuf and Alex, Tolga Bey the estate agent, Hatice Abla at the market.
- Week 3: moving in, then the new commute, Feriköy → Taksim (-(y)A / -DAn). New voices: Serkan, Ömer and Elif downstairs, and Maria Teyze upstairs.
- Week 4: far from family, a new family. Zafer Hoca's class project: everyone draws a family tree (aile ağacı) and presents it during the week. Family words come only through the existing cast, with no new characters (the family-visit idea was dropped on 2026-10-09).
- Week 5: time. Nima's watch is still on Tehran time (30 minutes ahead), so Nima is early or late everywhere (Ünite 5 A, and the workbook's *saat farkı* page). Past-tense stories come midweek (5 B), and a surprise birthday falls on Saturday (5 C): **Maria Teyze turns 80** on Sat 10 Oct (Nima finds out by accident in s1e27). Back this week: Kırlangıç and Rayan.
- Week 6: the best of Feriköy. Nima and Dilnoza write a guide for new students ("Feriköy'ün en iyi yerleri"), one place a day, located (yer-yön, -ki) and compared (daha, en). New and back this week: Berfin (her café is now in Feriköy), and Gülten Hanım, the florist on the corner (decided in s1e32).
- Week 7 (review): exam prep for the A1 exam on Fri 30 Oct, in a cold flat (the boiler breaks on Monday and is fixed on Friday). A study group at an İBB library, flash cards, a mock exam that goes badly (Wed), speaking practice with the neighbours; each day leans on one unit (Mon Ü1 … Fri Ü5, Sat Ü6 at Berfin's quiz night).
- Week 8 (exam week): Ömer rehearses his 29 Ekim song all week; Thursday is 29 Ekim (his kindergarten sings at the belediye's celebration in the Feriköy park by day, the Bosphorus lights at night); the A1 exam on Friday; a Saturday trip across the water to Kadıköy, looking ahead to A2. The exam results are not given in A1: they arrive in A2's first week.

**Language-neutral rule (decided 2026-10-09).** Nima is personalized per learner: `personalizeStr` swaps the name and the `Tahran` token with its suffixes, and nothing else. So Nima's lines, and lines about Nima, never claim a country, a nationality or a language. Only the hometown token says where Nima is from. The Persian bridge lives in the `fa` translations and in the Persian-speaking cast (Parvaneh, Kırlangıç, Farzad, Reza Bey); Nima never slips into Persian. Two exceptions are kept by the owner's choice: the cat call "Pişi" (s1e23) and the stated half hour of the Tehran-time watch (week 5), both true for every Iranian hometown.
