# Kumru: agent guide

*Kumru* (Kumru · Turkish Notebook) is a practice app for a Turkish course: word cards, grammar lessons, a skill path and a daily story series on Sahne's paper-theatre stage, live at **https://turk.shab.boo**. It is named after the kumru (Eurasian collared dove); its mascot and narrator is **Kerim**, a kumru, called "she". Her name comes from *yâ kerim*, the Turkish folk name for the kumru, after the sound of its coo. [docs/SPECS.md](docs/SPECS.md) is the plan, [docs/SPECS_API.md](docs/SPECS_API.md) every route and [docs/SPECS_SCHEMA.md](docs/SPECS_SCHEMA.md) every table, document and file. A route, table or field that is not in them does not ship, and they change in the same commit as the code.

The page boots on `window.kumru.host` (`app/host/index.ts`): content comes from static files; signed out, progress lives on the device under `kumru:progress:anon`; signed in, progress and tutor sync with the server. The app was called YaKerim, on another domain, until it moved here; the Cloudflare resources kept their names (below).

## Repo rules

These bind every change, by people and by agents.

- **The course data is private.** `data/{cards,cards-extra,grammar,path,syllabus}` are derived from the books and live in the private course repo (`kumru-data/` of `mehrad77/yakerim`). They are git-ignored here and filled by `scripts/data.mjs` (README.md, *Data*): linked from `$KUMRU_DATA` or the one-line `.kumru-data` file, or copied from `data-sample/`. Never `git add -f` them, never copy their text into a tracked file (a doc, a test, a fixture, `data-sample/`), and never commit a `yk-s-*` file or a `content/` path. CI fails on any of these and runs gitleaks. `data-sample/` holds only original text, written for this repo.
- **Never invent book text**, and never add book text here: anything from the books belongs in the course repo.
- **Sahne is a submodule** at `sahne/` (https://github.com/mehrad77/sahne): the engine, the kit, the Turkish plugin, the story format and checks, and the gallery. Change Sahne in its own repo, then move the submodule. Its story tools read Kumru's content through `SAHNE_CONTENT` (an absolute folder: `data/` on the real data; `scripts/gates.mjs` sets it). After a Sahne change that touches `sahne-gallery/thumbs.mjs` or the stage, regenerate the thumbnails (`SAHNE_CONTENT=$PWD/data node sahne/packages/sahne-gallery/thumbs.mjs`) and commit `data/thumbs/`.
- **Ids are permanent.** Never rename a card, lesson, skill, section, story, character or place id: add a new one and retire the old. Learners' progress points at them.
- **Every generator has `--check`**, run by `pnpm check`: the brand files (`scripts/kumru_icons.py`), the avatar art and cast parts (`scripts/avatar-art.ts`, `scripts/cast-parts.ts`), the extras and the thumbnails (Sahne's). The book generators (cards, path, path sentences, grammar lessons) are the course repo's, with their own `--check` there.
- **Docs follow the code**, in the same commit. `ROADMAP.md`, cited by milestone (`ROADMAP.md M8`), is the owner's roadmap, private in the course repo. Never renumber a section that code or other docs cite (`SPECS.md §11.5`, `SPECS_API.md §11a`).
- **Verify in the browser pane yourself**, with a seeded account (`pnpm seed`, then `?as=ada`), at phone size; never ask a person to check by hand.
- **Commits** are conventional: `feat(kumru): …`, `fix(worker): …`, `docs: …`.

### Production

The site is live and learners use it, so these bind every agent:

- **Never run ad-hoc SQL against the production D1.** The one documented exception is the statement that grants the owner role (*Production data* below). Everything else goes through a route or a migration in `worker/migrations/` (append-only; never edit or rename a migration).
- **Never deploy by hand** unless asked; CI deploys `main` (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`). Learner data is never part of a deploy.
- **The Cloudflare names stay** from the YaKerim days: the Worker `yakerim` (`wrangler.jsonc` `name`), the D1 database `yakerim`, the R2 bucket `yakerim-backups`. Renaming any of them makes a new, empty resource.
- **Stay inside Cloudflare's free tier.** The limits and the periodic check (`/kumru-costs`) are in [docs/COSTS.md](docs/COSTS.md). Nothing polls in the background.
- **Never `confirm()`** and never a one-tap delete: destructive actions go through `confirmSheet` (`app/ui/sheet.ts`).
- Backups land nightly in `yakerim-backups` (30 days kept). There is no written restore procedure yet; never improvise one.
- Secrets exist only in Cloudflare (`wrangler secret put`) and in GitHub Actions secrets (`CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN`, `KUMRU_DATA_TOKEN`). `wrangler.jsonc` holds only public `vars`.

### Shell

- zsh on macOS: quote globs (`rg -g '*.ts'`); `sed` is BSD (`sed -i ''`, no `\b`), so prefer `perl -pi -e`. Never `npx tsc` (it installs an unrelated package); `pnpm check` type-checks.
- A busy :8787 or `preview_start` failing with `getcwd` means an orphaned server: `lsof -iTCP:8787 -sTCP:LISTEN`, kill it, start again. Old `/assets/*` means an old service worker: `?fresh`, or unregister it. Turnstile cannot be passed in the pane on production; test sign-up locally, where it is off.
- A hidden browser pane pauses animation and timers: stage checks look frozen and screenshots come back empty.

## Host contract

`app/host/index.ts` exposes what the page used on claude.ai (docs/SPECS.md section 6); the account routes are the account feature's own client (`app/features/account/api.ts`), no longer on the host:

| Call | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `db.collection(name).orderBy(field).onSnapshot(ok, err)` | Loads `data/<name>.<hash>.json` once via `data/manifest.json`; fires once. `contrib`, `votes`, `tutorlab` are empty; `profiles` is the Friends payload (`GET /api/friends`), refreshed on focus (at most every 10 s) and every 2 minutes while Friends is open. |
| `db.doc("data/users/<me>/progress" \| ".../tutor")` | Signed in: `get` loads the server copy merged with any unsent save; `set` saves with `baseRev` (`app/host/sync.ts`); `onSnapshot` delivers a merged copy after a 409 or a pull on focus. Signed out: `get` is empty and `set` rejects `unauthorized`. |
| `db.doc("profiles/<me>").set(doc)` | `PUT /api/profile` with this week's key. |
| `user.id()` / `me()` / `canEdit()` | From `GET /api/me` once per page (the cached `kumru:me` when offline); `canEdit` is true for `role = 'owner'`. `null` only before sign-in, when the page shows the gate (no anonymous use). |
| `user.can("data.write")` | true when signed in (the Friends profile). Nothing else is granted on this host. |
| `ai` | `null`: the tutor, placement writing sample and talk use their offline paths. |
| `owner` | The Owner tab (`app/features/owner`, Preact; the old People tab is gone). Friends and classes are not on the host: they are the friends and classes features' own clients (`app/features/friends/api.ts`, `app/features/classes/api.ts`). Web Push reminders are the reminders feature's own client (`app/features/reminders/api.ts`; the service worker shows them). |
| `lib(name)` | The lazy TypeScript bundle `app/lib/<name>.ts` as an ES module: `import()` of its hashed file (`app/host/lib.ts`), once per page; a failed import is retried on the next call. Precached by the service worker, so it works offline. |
| `update()` | Activates a waiting service worker and reloads. The page shows *Reload* (`actionToast`) on the `kumru:update` event. |

Events the host sends the page: `kumru:update` (new version or 426), `kumru:signedout` (401), `kumru:notice` (a message to toast). `window.kumru.merge.progress` / `.tutor` are `shared/progress.ts` and `shared/tutor.ts`; the app merges every server copy into its live state with them (`app/features/progress/boot.ts`).

**Progress v5.** `progressDoc()` stamps `confAt[card]` and `at.{nick,char,profile,prefs,pos,placed}` at save time by comparing with what was last loaded or saved (`stampChanges`, `app/features/progress/model/doc.ts`), so no setter has to remember to. The merge rules are in docs/SPECS.md section 9.5 and tested in `shared/merge.test.ts`.

Host modules are TypeScript (`app/host/*.ts`), imported by `app/main.ts` and bundled by Vite into `assets/*.js`. The service worker is `app/host/sw.ts`, a second Vite entry written to `/sw.js`; the build fills in its `"__BUILD__"` placeholder with the version and precache list. A lazy bundle is registered in `app/host/lib.ts` (`LIBS`).

**The artifact is frozen.** Its content was dumped into `data/` on 2026-10-06 at page version v46 (`1791192848-9445`); the artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/RTssPEYEnbVgkE41eF5tUK) is no longer published to, and no ArtifactData write is ever made again. Learner data is not migrated (fresh start).

## Users

Two segments shape every design decision. They are mostly the same people in two situations.

| Segment | Who | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Persian-speaking students in Istanbul** | Adult international students, mostly from Iran, on a Turkish course (TÖMER-style classes) from A1 to B1. Persian is their first language; English is the UI language and a second language for most. | Persian beside every Turkish line, not only English. The book's order, terms and notation (-DA, -(I)yor), so the app matches class. Notes on where Persian works the same way and where it differs (ezafe, را, می‌). Their own city in stories (Tehran is the default hometown). |
| **Phone learners on the move** | The same learners on a metro, bus or ferry, or in a queue: one hand, 2–5 minutes at a time, often no sound or a noisy carriage, interrupted at any moment, on a small screen. | One idea per screen and no scrolling inside a step. The main button at the bottom, in thumb reach. Progress saved after every step, so leaving mid-session loses nothing. Nothing that requires listening (`listen` falls back to `type` without a Turkish voice). Short reading. |

What follows for the page:
- **Path lessons are light.** A lesson node teaches 2–3 `lite` cards with a check after each (see *Path, XP and friends*). The full lesson, with every table and widget, is for reading at home: the unit sheet's Lessons segment and the *Full lesson* link.
- **Steps stay short.** A lesson node is about 8–10 steps and a words node about 17, roughly 3–4 minutes. Long text, long tables and multi-part widgets belong on the lesson page, not in a session step.
- **Persian is first-class.** New learner-facing text gets Persian as well as English. Inside Persian elements, Latin runs (suffixes, Turkish words) are isolated by `faTxt` (`app/core/i18n.ts`) so they keep their direction.

## Changing the page

1. Read `app/README.md` (and `worker/README.md` for server work): the feature anatomy, the `WebFeature` / `ServerFeature` contracts, slots and the import boundaries. A screen is a feature under `app/features/<id>/`, with its server half in `worker/features/<id>/` and its contract in `shared/<id>.ts` (same id in all three).
2. Edit the feature. Find code with `grep`/`rg` over `app/`; the feature's `README.md` says what it owns.
3. `pnpm check` and `pnpm test` (at the repo root). For behaviour changes, preview `kumru` with `?learner` or a seeded account and check the console. After changing Kerim's head, run `uv run scripts/kumru_icons.py` too.
4. Commit `feat(kumru): …`.

The current stories, talk and cast are a placeholder `stories` feature until the Sahne stories land; the avatar seam below stays. Code worth turning into packages later is listed in `sahne/packages/README.md`.

## TypeScript and the Sahne seams

All page code is TypeScript under `app/`. Vite bundles `app/main.ts` (and what it imports), so the output is deterministic and `scripts/check-build.mjs` compares two builds byte for byte.

- **Pure helpers** go in `features/<id>/model/<name>.ts` with a `<name>.test.ts` beside it, or in `app/lib/` when several features share them (`node --test`; Node 24 strips the types; `pnpm test` runs it).
- **Big things** only some screens need (the Sahne engine, kit and builder) get their own `app/lib/<name>.ts`, a dynamic `import()` chunk (`public/assets/<name>-<hash>.js`, in the service worker's precache list, `/assets/*` cached immutable), registered in `app/host/lib.ts` (`LIBS`) and loaded with `window.kumru.host.lib("<name>")` (`loadLib(name)` in `app/host/lib.ts`) when the screen first opens.
- `pnpm check` type-checks and lints `app/` (tests excluded from `tsc`: they stub browser globals). Imports use the `.ts` extension.

`app/lib/char.ts` is what kind of value a `char` is (`charKind`: `none`, `legacy` (the old eight indexes), `avatar` (a Sahne avatar document, shape only) or `bad`), `legacyChar`, `charBytes` and `charStorable` (2 KB, sahne's `AVATAR_MAX_BYTES`). Its test checks that it agrees with sahne's own `isLegacyChar` and `isAvatarDoc`. `app/lib/avatar.ts` is a stub (`version`, `ready`) that becomes the Sahne bundle.

**Seams** (`app/ui/avatar/portrait.ts`; the art tables are `parts.ts` and `meArt.ts`, the `Avatar` component is `Avatar.tsx`). The M8 integration swaps `portrait.ts` and nothing else:
- `avatarPortrait(char, size, cls, label)` draws any learner's character: the user chip, the onboarding builder (preview, part options, the welcome-back step), Friends rows and sheets (`app/features/friends`), class members (`app/features/classes`) and the Owner's learners table. `myPortrait(size, cls)` is the signed-in learner where the page used the cast's `"ada"` slot (the Profile header: Ada's cast art before a character exists). `charKind` comes from `app/lib/char.ts`. `avatarUse()` loads the `avatar` bundle once (the Stories tab and the builder step call it) and, when the bundle exports `portrait(char, {size, cls, label})`, draws avatar documents with it; until then everything draws with the old parts (`meArt`).
- **Stories and talk are gone** until the Sahne stories land: the old reader, quiz, character sheet, talk screen, cast art and scenes were deleted. `app/features/stories` is the placeholder (the Stories tab and `/stories/<id>` show a "new stories are coming" card). The new `data/stories`, `characters` and `places` are in the repo and checked by `pnpm check` (the story check and `kumru_data.py`), but the page does not build or load them yet. The progress field `st` and the Friends profile's `storiesDone` are kept untouched.

## Changing content

Content lives only in `data/` (docs/SPECS.md section 5). The stories, characters, places and thumbnails are tracked here; the course folders are private (*Repo rules*). Every file is one document, pretty-printed JSON, so an edit to one card touches one file.

- **Card ids never change**, and a card is never deleted: learners' progress is keyed by id. A word that leaves the CSVs keeps its file with `retired: true`; `kumru_path.py --check` warns when a skill still names one.
- **Cards, lessons and the path** are private: change them in the course repo (its CSVs and generators), then point `.kumru-data` at it and run `pnpm check` here.
- **Cross-references.** Grammar ids are named in `stories.lessons` (and the stories' spots), the path's skills and the `DT` drill table (`app/features/drills/model/drills.ts`). `grammar_lessons.py --check` and the story check catch any that dangle. Skill ids never change either.
- **Stories, characters and places**: write them as `docs/stories/STORIES_AUTHORING.md` says, then `pnpm check` (or `node sahne/packages/sahne-story/stories.ts --check` alone); the Sahne gallery (in the Sahne repo, with `SAHNE_CONTENT` pointing at this `data/`) plays every story at `#/stories/<id>`. Story, character and place ids never change; the old series' ids (`s01`–`s16`, `sc01`–`sc16`, `w1`–`w4`, `ada can dede deniz ela marti mert selin simit zeynep`) are never reused.

## Gotchas

- The Browser pane can't open `file://` pages. Use the dev server.
- `AI.fn` is `host.ai`, null on Cloudflare, so AI features show their offline fallback.
- The service worker is off in dev unless `?sw`; after testing with it, unregister it (DevTools, or `navigator.serviceWorker.getRegistrations()`) or it keeps serving the old build.
- Saves are debounced 1.2 s in the page and the Worker refuses a second save of the same document within 1 s (`429`, retried by the host); a test that checks the server right after an answer must wait for the save.
- The Worker's `compatibility_date` is capped by the workerd that `@cloudflare/vitest-pool-workers` bundles; a newer date fails the Worker tests at startup.
- Stories exist for A1 only (Season 1). Cards, grammar, drills and the verb widget cover A1–B1.

## Onboarding

`openOnboarding(mode)` (`app/features/onboarding`, a Preact flow; its README lists the steps) is one full-screen flow on the page's overlay stack: `full` for a new learner, `update` once for an existing learner (`onb < ONB_V`), `edit` and `place` from Profile. The UI is English only. Steps: name (Latin spelling, Persian names get a transliteration chip), avatar builder (drawn with the parts in `app/ui/avatar`), about you (hometown, job, hobby, why; `buildProfile` in `app/features/tutor` asks the AI to clean them up, offline keeps the raw text), self-report, adaptive quiz, writing sample, result, daily goal, then (new accounts only) the suggested class (504, 506 or none) and the daily reminder.

- **Placement is cautious.** (`app/features/placement`.) The quiz climbs and drops by unit (`plNext`), words are scored like any answer (+2 right, −3 wrong, so a quiz alone can't make a word known), and a lesson is marked learned only when its own drills were all right, at least two were asked, its unit passed and the unit is below the chosen position. The writing sample can only move the suggestion down, by at most three units. *Choose myself* can pick the suggested unit or lower, never higher; going further is the path's *Jump here*.
- **First-time learners can't skip.** `full` mode (no practice history yet) hides every skip link and doesn't register with the overlay stack, so Escape and the browser back button don't close it. The in-flow Back button still works. `update`, `edit` and `place` keep their skip links.
- **Hometown.** `personalizeStr` (`app/features/onboarding/personalize.ts`) swaps Tahran/Tehran/تهران in stories, the glossary keys and journal prompts, with Turkish suffixes. Job, hobby and why go only into AI prompts through `learnerBlurb`.
- **Gate.** Without a session boot shows only the sign-in gate (`app/features/account`, opened at the `gated` boot phase). `?fresh` and `?learner` land on it; use `?as=<seeded user>` or sign up locally (no Turnstile in dev). A `?learner` blob in `kumru:progress:anon` is still uploaded by the first signup.
- **Dev.** `/onboarding` (or the old `#onb`) forces the full flow. The dev mock learner has `onb: 3` (`ONB_V`), so onboarding does not open on its own. At v3 an existing learner sees only the *what's new* and goal steps (the goal is now XP a day).
- **Offline limit.** Without the AI, a Persian mention of the old hometown in an English note gets the city's Latin name, because no Persian spelling exists yet.

## Path, XP and friends

- **The path** (`app/features/path`, `model/road.ts`) is one road from A1 Unit 1 to B1 Unit 6. Each unit with content is a section of steps from `unitNodes` (`model/road.ts`): its grammar lessons, 2–6 batches of its core words (cards with `core:1`, the words the book lists; units fall back to all unit words until the flag is synced), a checkpoint and a review. Lessons are done when learned; the rest are stored in `progress.nd`. `S.pos` is the learner's place: later units are locked (with *Jump here*, a tested jump; `jumpHere`), earlier ones stay open as *placed past*, and their unmet words wait under *Catch up* in the Review sheet. Finishing a checkpoint completes the unit and advances `pos`. There is no *My level* setting: `myLevel()` is `S.pos.lv`.
- **The skill tree** (`app/features/path`: `model/tree.ts`, `course.ts`, `Tree.tsx`, `Puck.tsx`) replaces the book's units for the levels `data/path` covers (drafted for A1, A2 and B1: 18 sections, 195 skills, all 86 lessons in book order; a level the tree does not cover still uses the units). It is behind a flag while it is built: editors see it (unless `?tree=0`) and anyone can open the page with `?tree`; both are remembered in `localStorage` (`kumru:tree`). `S.tree` holds the `path` sections (a signal, loaded by `app/features/path`), `S.tp` the learner's progress (`progress.tree = {v:1, sk:{<skill id>:{l,at,stars,how}}, sec:{<section id>:{at,stars,how}}, mig, tests[]}`, where `how` is migrated, placed, test or cp). `skillParts` turns a skill into parts (its grammar lessons, then its words in lessons of up to 8 via `chunkWords`; each word lesson opens with a warm-up on up to 4 earlier words, `skillReview`), `treeFlow` gives each skill its state (done, cur, locked; the checkpoint opens after the section's skills) and `SecSection` / `SkillItem` / `openSkillSheet` draw it. Each skill may carry `sentences`: cards (`kind:"sentence"`, `src:"reading"`, ids `yk-s-<slug>`, files `data/cards-extra/yk-s-*.json`) whose Turkish text is a sentence from the books' reading practices, picked by `tools/kumru_path_sentences.py` (a sentence sits in the skill that introduces its last word). A word lesson ends with up to two *Build the sentence* steps from them (`lessonSentences` picks those that use the lesson's words) and a last mixed match round; the draft with translations to review is `data/path/sentences-draft.json`. A sentence card may carry `accept`, a list of patterns for other correct answers: `[Sen] kaç yaşındasın` (optional words), `(Teşekkür ederim|Teşekkürler)` (alternatives) and `<dün|bu akşam> geldim` (the parts in any order); the card's own `tr` is always accepted, case and punctuation are ignored, and every word of a pattern must come from the sentence (the tile bank is built from it). `tools/kumru_path_sentences.py` proposes the optional subject pronoun (Ben/Sen/Biz/Siz when the last word carries the person) and a few hand-written word orders, and `--check` compiles every pattern. In *Build the sentence*, `checkBuilt` (`app/features/practice/model/answers.ts`) answers exact, an accepted variant ("Your version works too"), one swap of two neighbouring words in a sentence of four or more words (counts, headed "Almost", with the swapped words dotted-underlined and the fixed sentence), the right words in another order, or wrong; a miss shows what the learner wrote with the wrong positions dotted-underlined. Decoy tiles never use a word of any accepted form, and tiles show lower case except one-word proper names from the Words deck. In the skill tree's own steps (`hint:true`, set in `skillQueue`; never checkpoints, Jump here, placement or quizzes) the Turkish sentence shown in the feedback has each known word dotted-underlined, and tapping one opens its meaning (`showGloss`, quiet, no voice; `hintedText`). The question side is hinted too: the words of the Persian prompt (and of the English line when the learner prefers English or both) that stand for a Turkish word are dotted-underlined, and tapping one shows that Turkish word with its meaning (`promptSegments` matches each hinted Turkish word's card meanings to prompt words, exactly or as a stem plus up to three letters; `hintedPrompt`). The meaning prompt of a type step and of a choice step that asks for the Turkish word is hinted the same way (`meaningNode(c,big,hint)`: a word card's own Turkish word shows in the popup). Letter tiles are a recall step for words the learner knows (confidence 5+): no empty slots, so the length and the word split are not given away, and longer words get more decoy letters (4 from 9 letters, 5 from 12). Multiple-choice never re-admits an option that means the same (it may then show fewer than four). A typed answer may also be another card's Turkish word for the same meaning (`synonymsOf` with `meaningClash`: bay and bey, Teşekkürler and Teşekkür ederim); the feedback then says it means the same. A forgiven slip's feedback uses its own encouragement (`NEAR_PRAISE`: "Az kaldı!", not a flawless-answer line). Hints are given freely in the tree's lessons and hidden in checkpoints, Jump here, placement and quizzes; they never appear on the answer tiles. `glossToken` finds the card by exact word, before an apostrophe or after stripping up to three endings (`buildGlossIndex` also indexes verb stems and the words in parentheses), `glossSegments` matches phrase cards first, and a sentence card may carry `gloss: {word: "meaning"}` for words the lookup cannot reach. A part runs as a node of type `skill` (`skillQueue`: a lesson's cards, or `wordLessonSteps`), a section's checkpoint as type `treecp` (`secWords`, `secLessons`); `finishNode` records both in `S.tp`. Passing the last section of a level moves `S.pos` to the next level (`treeAdvance`). Existing learners are migrated once (`treeMigrate`), placement and the old *Jump here* place skills (`treeMark`), a locked section offers *Test out* (`testOut`), and the Review sheet's *Unit words* row opens the unit word lists; the rules are in `docs/PROGRESS.md` section 8. A level goes live for everyone when its sections lose `"draft": true` (all of a level at once, checked by `kumru_path.py --check`); until then only editors and `?tree` see it.
- **Lesson nodes** teach the lesson's `lite` cards (`grammar` field; format in `docs/GRAMMAR_BRIEF.md`): each card (`C.card`) is followed by its check (`C.cardq`, the card's `q` or the lesson quiz item at the same index, options shuffled), then one drill and one sentence build, then `lessonDone` (learned at 50% right). Each card has a *Full lesson* button that opens the lesson page over the session. A lesson without `lite` falls back to the old single `read` step.
- **Words nodes** teach 5 words (`NODE_WORDS`) through `wordLessonSteps` (`app/features/practice/model/lessons.ts`): a teach card (the `learn` question, which records the word as met with `recordSeen`), then a check of the word before it (`choice`, Turkish → meaning), a match round of all five, and a write-it step per word (`type`, with one `listen` when the browser has a Turkish voice). Every word is asked three times. A step that names its word (`c`, with `dir` and the wrong options in `dpool`) is asked as written; the other session types still draw weighted at random. A miss goes through `requeue` (`model/requeue.ts`) and comes back at the end, labelled *Once more*, at most twice; a retry counts for neither stars nor XP, so stars are first-try accuracy. Wrong options never mean the same as the answer (`meaningClash`). While a word's score is under 5 its prompts show Persian and English, whatever the meaning-language setting is. Tested by `scripts/kumru_tree.test.cjs`.
- **Sessions.** A node runs on the practice runner (`app/features/practice`) with a fixed queue (`nodeQueue`, `app/features/practice/model/nodes.ts`; `kinds.ts` registers the driver that picks each step); `finishNode` stars it, awards XP and saves. The *Review* button on the current step opens timed focus sessions (weak spots, suffix drill, new words, quick review, fix my mistakes).
- **XP**: 1 per right answer, +10 for finishing a words node or a newly learned lesson, +20 for a checkpoint, +5 for a daily review. `xpDays` feeds the goal ring and the weekly leaderboard (`weekKey`).
- **Words and positions.** `docs/PROGRESS.md` is the source for confidence scores, what counts as known, placement, jumps and catch-up. Read it before changing any of them.
- **Migration.** `migrateProgress` runs once for saved docs without `v:3`: `pos` from the old level and start unit, finished units from the old 80% rule, XP from answers given.
- **Companion.** Kerim the kumru greets the learner in the Profile header (her line from `companionLine()` in `app/features/path/companion.ts`). The Path has no hero: it opens scrolled to the next step. See *Design → Kerim*.
- **Friends** (`app/features/friends`): follow by code or name, mutual follows are friends, weekly XP leaderboard, inbox, cheers (*Çok iyi!* / *Hadi!*), friend sheet. The **People** tab lists every `profiles` doc for editors.

The reader on `yeni.shab.boo` links to `/connect/yeni?return=<its page>&state=…&code_challenge=…&code_challenge_method=S256`; after the gate and a consent sheet the page calls `POST /api/yeni/codes` and goes back with `#yk_code=<code>&state=<state>` (`#yk_error=denied&state=…` on Cancel or closing the sheet). The reader trades the code with `POST /api/yeni/token`, then uses `GET`/`PUT /api/yeni/doc` and `DELETE /api/yeni/token` with the bearer token, cross-origin: those two paths alone send CORS, for the `YENI_ORIGINS` var (production `https://yeni.shab.boo`; `.dev.vars` adds `http://localhost:4330`), and answer flat `{ error: code }` bodies. The notebook is the `docs` row with `kind = 'yeni'` (≤ 512 KiB, never parsed); tokens live hashed in `yeni_tokens`, one-time codes in `yeni_codes` (`worker/features/yeni/routes.ts`, migration 0007, tests in `worker/features/yeni/yeni.test.ts`). To try it: `pnpm dev` here and `pnpm dev` in `apps/reader/` with `localStorage['yi-dev-kumru'] = 'http://localhost:8787'`, then press *Connect with Kumru* in the reader's Ayarlar (a hand-made `/connect/yeni` URL needs `state`, `code_challenge` and `code_challenge_method=S256`, and its code is useless without the reader's verifier). `smoke/connect.spec.ts` covers the gate, consent, Cancel and a bad return; it calls the bearer routes from Node, because Chrome's local-network rule blocks a page that Playwright stubs (not served from loopback) from fetching a loopback port. A real reader on `localhost:4330` is not affected. A new account that signs up on `/connect/yeni` gets onboarding on its next visit, not over the consent sheet (`maybeOnboard`).
- **Book bridge** (docs/SPECS.md §10.3 and §10.10). `app/features/yeni` (`book.ts`, `model/book.ts`) turns the reader's notebook (`notebook()` in its `api.ts`, fetched only for accounts with a reader connection, on Path or Profile becoming visible, at most every 10 minutes) into the `book` model: the Path's *In the book: …* line back to the reader page, the checkpoint or tested-jump offer once a book unit is marked done (docs/PROGRESS.md §5), the Yeni banner at the end of Profile, and the `book` summary that rides with progress saves while the learner shares book progress with a class (`class_members.share_book`, off by default; teacher strip: `ClassBook.tsx`, drawn into the class pages of `app/features/classes`). `/unit/<REF>` opens a unit on the Path; the reader links there. Data only flows reader → Kumru.
