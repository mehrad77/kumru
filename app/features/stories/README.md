# stories

Kerim's stories on Sahne's stage (docs/stories/, sahne/packages/sahne, sahne/packages/sahne-story): the Stories tab (the home tab: the page opens on it), the reader at `/stories/<id>` and the Path's "out today" banner. No server half: the stories, characters and places are static collections, and what a learner did is their progress document's `st`.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tabs | `stories` (`/stories`, order 5, first in the tab bar; `core/startup.ts` opens the page on it unless a link asks for another screen): a timeline of every story out, week by week, each with its thumbnail (`Thumb.tsx`), its episode number (Bölüm), done with its stars, locked, or marked as today's; the learner's next story is drawn large with its summary and Read, and the tab opens scrolled to it; the story coming tomorrow closes the line (`StoriesTab.tsx`). Before Season 1's launch day it is the "coming" card for everyone but editors and `?stories` |
| Deep links | `/stories/<id>` opens the reader once the collections are in and the story is out for this learner (`index.ts`, `needs`/`exists`/`gone`) |
| Who sees what | `state.ts`: a story is out when it is not a draft, its `releaseDate` is on or before today in Istanbul by the server's clock (`host/transport.ts serverNow`), and its season has launched (`shared/stories.ts SEASON_LAUNCH`; Season 1 opened 2026-10-28, its last stories releasing daily to 31 Oct). Editors and anyone who opened the page with `?stories` (remembered under `kumru:stories`; `?stories=0` hides it) see every story in the build: production leaves drafts out (`scripts/vite/page.ts`). `?today=YYYY-MM-DD` stands in for today in dev builds. **In order:** a story opens once every story before it in release order is finished (`readable`, `shared/stories.ts isReadable`); `open.ts` refuses a locked one with a toast that names the story to read first (and an editor's "Open anyway") |
| Reader | `open.ts` opens the full-screen page (core/overlay.ts: back and Escape); the stage fills its top edge to edge, under a dark strip in the notch, with a small round back button over it and no title. `reader.ts` builds it around the stage from the lazy `player` bundle (`app/lib/player.ts`: engine, Turkish plugin, kit with Kerim and the animals, the story's set families, the adapter, `storyPlayer`). Log mode on an upright phone, theatre mode sideways or by the "Bubbles on stage" setting (`stage.setBubbles`). The log keeps its newest line in view, larger than the earlier ones, and the footer (Devam, the reply card, the quiz) is pinned to the screen's bottom. Every newcomer line is the learner's card (Söyle; the microphone where the week turns `aloud` on and recognition really works here: `voice.ts` hides it on browsers with the API but no service behind it, while the microphone is blocked, and after a try failed with `unsupported` or `network`, remembered as `kumru:story:nomic` until a later try hears something); a choice shows its options shuffled and stores the authored index. Kerim's spot is a card in the log with the lesson's link. Translations follow the help language (`app/core/lang.ts helpLangs`): Persian, English, both, or none, in the log, the reply cards, the spots and the quiz. Tap-to-translate (always on): the meaning in the help language (English for none), from the story's `gl`, then the word cards (`loadGlossModel` from the practice feature), in the words feature's popup. Without WebGL2 the story reads in the log over a still with the place's name |
| After the story | the quiz (3 questions, shuffled; stars are the first-try answers, at least one), the follow-up dialogue in the log, then the record (`model/record.ts`): `st[id] = {done, stars (best), reads, at, picks (ok choices), ch (choice and act id → pick, follow-ups too), fu}`, +10 XP the first time, an active day and a `log` entry with `st: 1`. Flags (callbacks in later stories) and warmth are derived from `ch` (sahne-story `flagsOf`, `warmthOf`), never stored |
| Personalisation | the onboarding feature's `personalize`/`personalizeStr`: Nima becomes the learner's name, Tahran their hometown, in every text, place name and caption |
| Slots | fills `profileCards` (order 65: the Stories card, `Settings.tsx`: bubbles on stage, and saying your lines aloud where the microphone works) and `pathBanners` (order 5: `Banner.tsx`, the story out today with its thumbnail; a learner who is behind is sent to their next story first) |
| Thumbnails | `data/thumbs/<id>.webp`, each story's opening scene, written by `sahne/packages/sahne-gallery/thumbs.mjs` (`--check` in `pnpm check`) and served from the manifest's `thumbs` base (`thumbUrl` in `state.ts`) |
| Device keys | `kumru:stories` (the preview switch), the reader's settings (`prefs.ts`, set in the Stories card): `kumru:story:aloud`, `kumru:story:view2` (`auto`, `log` or `theatre`; the first version's `story:view` and `story:translate` are no longer read), and `kumru:story:nomic` (`voice.ts`) |
| CSS | `stories.css` (`st-*`) |

## What this feature calls in other features

`personalize`, `personalizeStr` and `studentName` from onboarding; `openLesson` from grammar; `loadGlossModel` from practice; `showGloss` and `closeGloss` from words; `addXp`, `bumpDay`, `saveProgress` and `todayKey` from progress.
